Capsule shell colour

A design lever, not a manufacturing detail.

These are the shell options available to us: single colour, two tone, and clear. The same shell shows through the blister window and sits loose in both tins, so one colour per product carries across all three capsule formats.

Suggested order of work: assign a shell colour to each capsule product first, then design the blister card, tin and container around it.

yours Nothing about shell colour is locked.

Already decided

So nobody re-opens these.

  • Gummies stay in plastic.Tin was ruled out for gummies.
  • 25 is DTC, 14 is retail.For both substrates. The blister is the one exception: 14 day for Stasis, 12 day for Thesis, 7 day for the Day + Night set.
  • Every capsule product is two per serving.So 25 day is 50 capsules, 14 day is 28 and 12 day is 24, in every capsule format.
  • The refillable container is DTC only.Travel tin and blister are retail only, and it will be one or the other, not both.
  • The pouch is the DTC refill.First order ships container plus pouch. From the second order on, the pouch sells alone. It never goes on a retail shelf by itself; a smaller version is likely inside the retail tin.
  • Blister foil colour is fixed.Everything else on the blister unit is open.
