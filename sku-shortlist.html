Stasis & Thesis · Retail packaging · The ten SKUs

SKU Shortlist

Owner: MollyUpdated 4 Nov 2026Ten SKUs to final direction

The situation in one paragraph

Stasis and Thesis are two supplement brands from the same company. Adults take capsules. Kids take gummies. Both brands are moving from online only into retail stores, which means new packaging. Dayjob is taking ten SKUs through to final design. A SKU is one product in one pack, for example Stasis Day in a blister pack. Ten is not enough to design every pack we sell, so the ten have to be chosen to establish the rules that let the rest of the range be designed later without starting over. This page is the ten and the reasoning behind them.

The goal is to leverage Dayjob's design skills across enough SKUs to build out a complete packaging system, not ten individual packs.

So these ten are selected to give Dayjob the most complete understanding of that system across three dimensions: format, variant logic, and system rules. The test for each SKU is not commercial priority. It is what the SKU teaches the agency that no other SKU on the list does, so the rest of the range can be designed from the system without a second engagement.

FormatThe physical pack a product comes in. A gummy jar in two sizes, a refillable metal container for home, and for retail capsules one of two options still being decided: a small travel tin with a pouch, or a blister pack in a folded card. The ten include both retail options so the choice is made with designs in hand, not before.
Variant logicHow packs for related products relate to each other. The same product in two different packs. Two products in the same pack. A Day version next to a Night version. A set of two instead of one. A shorter pack next to a longer one.
System rulesThe rules that let the agency design the rest of the range later. How Stasis and Thesis look related but distinct. How Kids relates to adult. How the outer box carries the shelf face whichever retail option wins.

Products that need to be in this design system

  • Stasis Day, Stasis Night Adult capsules. Taken with stimulant medication, one in the morning, one at night. Sold singly and as a set. Both on the ten.
  • Stasis Kids Day, Stasis Kids Night, Thesis Kids Steady Focus Gummies for children. Kids Day is on the ten in the 25 serving DTC jar and Kids Night in the 14 serving retail jar, so the pair and both sizes get drawn. Thesis Kids Steady Focus follows the Stasis Kids jar once that is set.
  • Thesis Clarity, Stress Reset, Neuroprotection, Motivation Adult capsules under the Thesis brand. Clarity and Stress Reset are on the ten. Neuroprotection and Motivation follow the same rules once those two are set.
